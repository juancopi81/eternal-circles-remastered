Eternal Circles

Drag a melody into a circle and it turns forever.

your cursor is a lantern · headphones, please

A remaster of Eternal Circles (2020) by Juan Carlos Piñeros · melodies by MusicVAE · piano: Salamander Grand (CC BY 3.0)

How to play

drag a melody onto a circle
it loops there, two bars at a time
click a melody
listen to it once
click the dark centre
retrograde: the melody runs backwards
double-click a circle
let it go quiet
scroll over a circle
its volume
click the words under a circle
its instrument, and how its bells answer
move the lantern
the voices you light come closer

Each circle is a score: the angle is time, the distance from the centre is pitch. The magenta arcs are the melody (Pärt's M-voice). The golden circles are the notes of the tonic triad, and each golden bead is a bell (the T-voice) placed on the triad note just above or below the melody note it answers. The eight melodies are eight steps through the latent space of MusicVAE, from melody A to melody B.
